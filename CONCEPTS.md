# CONCEPTS
- Page: a sheet with title, body, paper, type, ink, size. Not a document.
- Library: recency stack (Today / Yesterday / This week / Earlier). No folders.
- Desk: phone-shaped shell on cream wood. Do not stretch to a three-pane app.
- Style: belongs to the page. Last choice is the next blank sheet’s default.
- Focus: chrome fades. Tap the top of the sheet to come back.
- Mark: cream sheet, red margin, serif V. Stamp on the library, not a wordmark. Not on the editor.
- Local: localStorage key `vellum-pages-v1`. No accounts, no sync.
